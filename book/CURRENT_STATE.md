# Scientific currency and general-book evidence policy

This **general Rydberg science textbook** must remain usable for many atomic species, experiments, neutral-atom platforms and research topics. It does not endorse any one laboratory's selected frequency, metasurface design, optical receiver or manuscript as universal theory.

For the verified project-specific research history, follow the [current evidence portal](../docs/research/CURRENT_PROJECT_EVIDENCE_PORTAL.md), [full timeline and source status](../docs/research/PRIOR_CURRENT_STATE_HISTORY.md), and [source-backed results](../docs/results/KEY_FINDINGS.md). Earlier flawed/partial HFSS and laboratory cases remain visible in those project folders rather than masquerading as textbook material.

## How to keep a textbook scientifically current

- Verify claims against primary peer-reviewed papers, the latest publisher correction and authoritative atom-data references.
- Record when a preprint becomes peer reviewed and whether numerical/sensor demonstrations were experimentally validated.
- Recheck quantum numbers, constants, phase/handedness and unit conventions against the exact software version.
- Keep foundational physics independent of project-local hardware, suppliers, unpublished data or output filenames.
- External funding or collaborator discovery belongs in the separate [research and opportunity monitor](../automation/README.md).
