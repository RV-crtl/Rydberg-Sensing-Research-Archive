# Research communication and evidence maturity

**Part:** Research practice · **Type:** educational background, not new experimental data.

## Core explanation

Scientific novelty needs positioning against existing experimental full-polarimetry results. The 2024 Elgee team measured a full 3D RF polarisation ellipse using orthogonal local oscillators, and later 2026 works explored complete spectroscopic polarimetry and the multi-mJ origin of complex AT spectra. This programme's distinct research contribution is a simulated, information-aware switchable RF front end and its limitations, not priority over all Rydberg polarimetry. Preserve both successful periodic metrics and poor finite calibration-transfer results.

## Worked learning / laboratory exercise

Create a claim table mapping each manuscript sentence to (1) measured, (2) HFSS-native, (3) algebraic-derived, (4) design/proposed or (5) published externally. Include source paths and exact figure provenance.

## Self-test

**Question:** Is a full-rank 6×4 HFSS basis proof of an experimentally demonstrated rubidium full-Stokes receiver?

<details><summary>Reveal answer and scientific reasoning</summary>

No. The source supports observability under the simulated field model; an experimental Rb cell needs calibrated optical transfer and hardware/finite-array validation.

</details>

## Red flags and diagnosis

Overclaiming originality, fabricated hardware, atomic signal recovery or continuous bandwidth.

## Validity and reflection

- Identify each approximation or model limitation before reusing the worked example.
- Identify which physical measurement or native HFSS field could falsify the argument.
- Document units, state basis, time convention, calibration reference, exact code and source versions.
- Check sensitivity to numerical errors and state a quantitative acceptance gate before declaring a result.
