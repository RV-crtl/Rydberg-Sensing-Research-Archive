# Rydberg research repository — start here

**Two different destinations serve two different purposes.**

**1. [General Rydberg Systems Engineering textbook](../book/README.md):** foundational graduate physics and science, 36 general chapters with equations, practice and literature. It is not a report on our particular laboratory. [Interactive book](../site/academy.html) · [numerical lab](../site/lab.html) · [editorial completion requirements](../book/EDITORIAL_STANDARD.md).

**2. [Current and historical project evidence portal](research/CURRENT_PROJECT_EVIDENCE_PORTAL.md):** the right source for what was actually simulated, measured, proposed, published or rejected in the Rb-87 microwave Stokes work. Version precedence is based on model/observable and scientific validation, not filenames or upload dates.
