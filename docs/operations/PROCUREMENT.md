# Procurement, quotations and equipment provenance

This section separates **confirmed use in experimental reports**, **equipment proposed or costed** and **orders awaiting invoice/receipt evidence**. It makes no claim that every listed component was purchased.

The imported `Portable Rydberg System` design presentation includes a photodetector purchase **proposal**, not a receipt. Its quoted USD prices are historical proposal figures and not verified paid costs. These are recorded in [`BOM.csv`](BOM.csv).

The following records have not yet been identified in the accessible project exports and should be imported via the documented [intake method](../handover/SUCCESSOR_ONBOARDING.md): supplier receipts, actual purchase orders, order dates, shipping, tax invoices, serial numbers, lab asset IDs, handover/custody confirmation and warranty sheets. Store identifiable or commercially sensitive documents in a restricted repository or encrypted institutional records system, linking to an access-controlled location here.

**Purchasing ledger:** [BOM.csv](BOM.csv), with status and evidence fields; blank amounts intentionally mean *unverified*, not zero cost.
