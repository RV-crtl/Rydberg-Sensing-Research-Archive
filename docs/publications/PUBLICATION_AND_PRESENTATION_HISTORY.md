## Editorial evolution and scientific contributions

1. The interim thesis emphasised atomic transitions, 780/480 nm optical sensing, RF antennas, magnetic bias and first laboratory polarisers.
2. The initial polarisation programme identified a core information gap: linear-only readout samples the Poincaré equator and cannot independently recover S3.
3. APL Photonics-format papers explored CP selectors, finite-aperture concepts, aperture laws, HFSS candidates, Stokes reconstruction and research figures.
4. The later paper adopted **measurement-basis/information-aware** language rather than suggesting full hardware atomic polarimetry: two copper faces, switching, six states, full-rank simulated Stokes basis, condition number and rigorous finite/mesh qualifications.
5. Subsequent IEEE Access revisions refined the title and abstract to accurately match simulations; added explicit contributions, a clear condition-number explanation, assumptions, model limitations, engineering significance, nonspecialist exposition and publication-quality figures.
6. Changes included consistent figure labels/subcaptions and numbering, real HFSS-origin data rather than synthetic illustration, source-linked Jones/Stokes plots, and preservation of complete editable LaTeX files instead of embedding a static PDF in TeX.

## Figures and reproducibility policy

Historical request: Figure 1 an authentic annotated optical bench image; Figure 2 physically accurate atomic/RF schematic and energy ladder; single/double-column layout chosen for legibility; HFSS copper drawings must match switch topology; all plots must have axis units and readable captions; no decorative/unrelated photographs. The existing [figure provenance](../results/FIGURE_PROVENANCE.md) points to the current paper's source and data. Historical figure-atlas PDFs remain discoverable through the source index until imported.

**Research article claims must not exceed results:** numerical full rank is not a completed atomic measurement; finite 3×3 differs from infinite periodic calibration; ideal PIN bridges are not validated switches; no manufactured full-Stokes single-board demonstrator is asserted.

## Reviewer and supervisor evidence

Rubric priorities: **20% research contribution, 20% methodology, 30% engineering research and 30% research communication**. Evidence should include literature gap, controlled methodology, real project source files, technical evaluation and publication/academic communication. The published rubric itself is retained under [governance](../../governance/), and its [assessment mapping](../traceability/RUBRIC_MAPPING.md) identifies where examples are located. Draft review scores are *not* publication decisions or formal final marks.

## Bibliography governance

Keep peer-reviewed works, government/institute reports and reliable primary references traceable. Distinguish independently published papers from unpublished posters or theses. Do not cite the user's unpublished manuscript as peer-reviewed previous work. For any self-citation, verify full author list, title, journal/conference, year, DOI and publisher access licence.
